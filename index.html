Our Story

Rooted in
Russian Heritage

✦

Golden Eagle was born from a deep love for the rich culinary tapestry of Russia — a land where food is ceremony, warmth, and storytelling woven into every dish. Founded by Chef Alexei Volkov in 1994, we have brought the grandeur of the imperial table to modern dining.

Every recipe carries the soul of a grandmother's kitchen, refined through classical technique and the finest seasonal ingredients sourced from both Russian farms and the world's best purveyors. From the golden depths of our Borscht to the delicate layers of our Beef Stroganoff, each plate is a letter home.
